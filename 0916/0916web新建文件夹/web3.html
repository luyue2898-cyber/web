Elvis Presley

Who is Elvis?

Elvis Presley was one of the best-known American singers of the 20th century. As a cultural icon, Elvis's music gained huge popularity worldwide, and he is often called "the King of Rock and Roll". He was born in Tupelo, Mississippi.

His music and life

Presley received his first guitar as a gift from his mother on his 11th birthday in 1946 and had his first taste of musical success a few years later when he won a talent show at Humes High School in Memphis.
