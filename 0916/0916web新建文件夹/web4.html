Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart

Mozart (January 27, 1756 – December 5, 1791) was a genius Austrian composer born in Salzburg on January 27, 1756.

Life

Mozart was born in Salzburg on January 27, 1756. He learned piano and violin from his father, a court musician, and began composing at the age of five. Between 1764 and 1765, he first learned how to write symphonies from Bach, which inspired him to leave a large number of symphonic works. In Vienna, Mozart met the 14‑year‑old Beethoven in 1784 and devoted himself to teaching him.

Death

Mozart died suddenly of illness at about 00:55 on December 5, 1791. His unfinished work, the Requiem, was completed by Franz Xaver Süssmayr.

Music

He composed more than 600 works covering a wide range of genres including operas, symphonies, marches, orchestral dances, piano concertos, violin concertos, church vocal pieces, cantatas, and masses, leaving them for later generations.

Legends Surrounding Mozart's Death

There is a story that it rained and thundered on the day of Mozart’s funeral, but according to the New Grove, it was actually a completely cloud‑less, clear day.
