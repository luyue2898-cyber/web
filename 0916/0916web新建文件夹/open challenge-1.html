Smartphone

A smartphone is a wireless mobile phone combined with a computer. Equipped with a mobile operating system, a scaled‑down version of the PC operating system, it has almost all PC functions such as internet search, e‑mail, simple document editing, camera, audio and video playback.

History

The first smartphone is believed to be Simon. Designed by IBM in 1992, it was exhibited as a concept product at COMDEX held in Las Vegas, Nevada, USA that year.

Android

Android is a software stack and mobile operating system that includes an operating system, middleware, user interface, and standard applications (web browser, email client, SMS, MMS, etc.) for portable devices including mobile phones.

iPhone

The iPhone is a mobile phone series announced by Apple on January 9, 2007. It was presented by Steve Jobs, one of Apple’s founders, at Macworld 2007 held in San Francisco, USA.
